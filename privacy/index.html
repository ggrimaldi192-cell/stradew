Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Stradew collects and how it is used. Stradew makes and sells digital products (planners, templates and printables) under three brands: Italian Kitchen Basics, Pagewise Planner and Coinleaf Budget. The same policy applies to all of them, to our Instagram and Pinterest accounts, and to the "Stradew Agency" app we use to manage those accounts.

Who we are

Stradew is run by Giuseppe Grimaldi. For any question about this policy or your data, write to giuseppe.grimaldi.dp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you buy on Whop. Payments are handled by Whop. We never see or store your card details. We keep a record of each sale (the product, the amount and Whop's order reference) to count our sales. Whop's own privacy policy covers the data you give to Whop.
  • When you comment on our Instagram posts. If you comment on one of our posts with the keyword shown in the post (for example "RECIPES"), our app reads the comment and sends you one private message with the link to the product. To do this and to avoid messaging you twice, we keep your Instagram user ID, your username, the comment text and the post it was made on.
  • When you message us. If you write to us on Instagram, Whop or by email, we keep the conversation so that we can answer you.
  • Pinterest. Our app only publishes our own pins on our own boards and reads the statistics of our own pins. It does not collect information about other Pinterest users.
  • Statistics. We read the totals that Instagram and Pinterest give us for our own posts (for example views, saves and shares). These numbers are not linked to individual people.

How we use it

  • to send you the product link you asked for in a comment;
  • to answer your messages and help you with a purchase;
  • to count our sales and see which posts work best.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising profiles, and we do not share it with anyone except the platforms that make the service work (Instagram/Meta, Pinterest and Whop).

How long we keep it

Instagram comment records are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Sale records are kept as long as we need them for our accounts and taxes. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

How to delete your data

Send an email to giuseppe.grimaldi.dp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram or Pinterest username, or the email you used on Whop. We delete everything we hold about you within 30 days and reply to confirm.

You can also remove our app's access to your account at any time. On Instagram, go to Settings → Apps and websites. On Pinterest, go to Settings → Security and logins → Apps.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Our products are bought by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
